# Initial architecture decision records

Date: 4 November 2026. These records separate approved constraints from conditional mechanisms. A proposed record is not an accepted hardware backend. References to specification sections mean the [unchanged v1.1 baseline](../requirements/Wired_Earphone_Module_Agent_Specification.md). Live sources and their limits are in [research](../research.md).

## ADR-001 Native interception versus overlays — proposed candidate

**Problem:** Suppress only earphone controls without harming shared Android mappings or audio. **Evidence:** Specification §§3, 6–7, 11; [Linux uinput](https://docs.kernel.org/input/uinput.html), [libevdev grab](https://www.freedesktop.org/software/libevdev/doc/latest/group__init.html), [AOSP layouts](https://source.android.com/docs/core/interaction/input/key-layout-files). The initial record preceded phone measurements; the [reference checkpoint](../discovery/reference-2026-11-04.md) now records a dedicated USB control collection, separate audio and root/kernel prerequisites. Actual service/proxy behavior remains unproven.

**Behavior:** Evaluate exclusive evdev grab plus uinput from a mountless service. Stage 1 eligibility and Stage 4 passthrough are mandatory. Unknown or unsuitable nodes remain untouched. **Alternatives:** Read-only observation cannot cancel; grab-only is a separate reduced all-control mode; scoped layout overlays and framework adapters are deferred. **Revisit:** Measured transport, service permissions, or mapping proves the candidate unsuitable; document evidence and obtain acceptance before adding dependencies or changing baseline scope.

## ADR-002 Proxy identity and mapping — validation strategy accepted; mapping pending

**Problem:** Identical Linux codes may map differently on a virtual device. **Evidence:** Specification §7; [AOSP layout lookup](https://source.android.com/docs/core/interaction/input/key-layout-files). **Behavior:** Mark virtual devices distinctly, exclude them from discovery, advertise only verified capabilities, require Android readiness and mapping evidence, and assign one repeat owner before grabbing. **Alternatives:** Blindly clone identity or assume a delay proves readiness; both are insufficient. **Revisit:** Stage 4 requires a narrowly documented identity-field copy or scoped mapping; an overlay would change backend classification and needs a separate accepted decision.

## ADR-003 Conservative enrollment — accepted project constraint

**Problem:** Names and allocated device paths can select unrelated devices or reject the same accessory after a new allocation. **Evidence:** Specification §§4, 7, 17; reference identifiers, capabilities and USB parentage are measured in the checkpoint, including changed HID/input instance suffixes with stable measured attributes; boot/duplicate behavior and production matching remain unproven. One controlled reconnect is retained and further physical repetitions are explicitly waived. **Behavior:** Combine stable measured identity, collection, capabilities, and parentage, then revalidate after open and reconnect. Do not persist event numbers, `inputN` or HID allocation suffixes as hardware identity. Reject ambiguity, mixed essential input, general keyboards, and proxies. Model-level and serial-specific scope must be distinguished. **Alternatives:** Name-only, port-only, event-number-only and path-wildcard-only matching are prohibited. **Revisit:** A new transport needs new measured identity evidence and preserved denials, never a wider wildcard.

## ADR-004 Shared action semantics — accepted project constraint

**Problem:** Playback, calls, and assistant can share a center signal. **Evidence:** Specification §5; [AOSP USB headset mappings](https://source.android.com/docs/core/interaction/accessories/headset/usb-device) are upstream references; the checkpoint now measures Center short/hold/double/cellular actions on one raw signal, volume pulse pairs during holds, and paused overlay-only behavior with unchanged media indices. **Behavior:** Block all and capability-driven Custom groups; disclose shared effects before activation. Unsupported distinctions retain the prior valid policy. **Alternatives:** Independent UI switches for one raw drop rule are misleading; gesture buffering and semantic injection are outside baseline. **Revisit:** Measured distinct signals support more independent groups, or a separately accepted semantic project has its own timing and compatibility tests.

## ADR-005 Independent supervision — accepted safety requirement; deadlines pending

**Problem:** A hang can retain an exclusive grab indefinitely. **Evidence:** Specification §§7, 13, 17. **Behavior:** Only the worker owns grab descriptors; a separate supervisor monitors real loop progress, bounds shutdown, rate-limits restart, and latches inactive after three failures per boot. Immediate pause and next-boot escape must precede the first physical prototype. **Alternatives:** Same-process watchdog or a heartbeat independent of forwarding cannot establish release. **Revisit:** Before Stage 4 choose measured deadlines and process identity/termination semantics; after fault injection refine bounds without weakening independent recovery. A host-tested state machine now implements the gate, deadlines, restart spacing, latch and unsafe terminal transitions. The real process adapter, measured deadlines and fault injection remain open.

## ADR-006 Durable policy and atomic generations — accepted storage choice; schema pending

**Problem:** Manager UI state cannot be the only durable policy and untrusted text must never execute. **Evidence:** Specification §§8–9; [KernelSU WebUI persistence caveat](https://kernelsu.org/guide/module-webui.html). **Behavior:** Plan `/data/adb/earkey_control/policy.json`, root-owned private storage, strict versioned JSON, atomic durable replacement, and worker acknowledgment of an exact generation. Keep durable pause/recovery state separately. No actual files or schema are installed. **Alternatives:** Shell-sourced config, sole localStorage, permissive schemas, and writable network control are rejected. **Revisit:** Stage 2 pins schema, size limits, migration rollback, and secure file-operation behavior; new managers remain adapters.

## ADR-007 Release identity, signing, and attestation — publication policy confirmed; release controls pending

**Problem:** Shipped bytes must match reviewed and physically accepted bytes without invented publisher identity. **Evidence:** Specification §§16, 18–20; [GitHub secure Actions](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/security/secure-use), [immutable releases](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/concepts/supply-chain-security/immutable-releases). **Behavior:** MIT/public source under AmanSikarwar is approved; release only after implementation and acceptance. Use an exact reviewed clean commit, pinned build inputs, deterministic packaging, SHA-256 manifest, two clean-build comparisons, dependency notices, available provenance, annotated tag and draft-first prerelease. Sign only with an established maintainer identity. **Alternatives:** Mutable assets, moved tags, source archives as installers, and rebuilding different bytes after device acceptance are rejected. **Revisit:** D08 must establish actual merge controls and signing/attestation availability before release; no release is eligible in Stage 0.

## Reference eligibility review — 5 November 2026

The measured USB HID consumer-control collection is separate from audio and from the observed phone control/switch/touch nodes. It has no observed axis or switch capability. Read-only evdev access, uinput presence/kernel configuration and enforcing-root metadata keep grab + uinput worth investigating. These are prerequisites, not demonstrated service permission or proxy equivalence.

At that checkpoint the decision was to retain the primary native candidate for investigation and host fixture design. Active interception was ineligible. Intended module-service access, real uinput readiness/classification/mapping, repeat ownership, passthrough and bounded recovery remain unproven. The Stage 1 unrelated-input baseline is waived under [D19](../project-contract.md#reference-unrelated-input-baseline-amendment--7-november-2026) with a Not run result. T11 still needs physical module evidence. Broader context and call coverage is incomplete and the candidate awaits enrollment. The 7 November decision below records the final Stage 1 disposition.

Scoped alternatives were reviewed on paper: grab-only cannot provide Custom forwarding and needs its own reduced-capability decision; overlays need exact layout/mount/reload proof; semantic injection is outside baseline. No measured native failure justifies switching to those alternatives. Safe inactivity is the current runtime outcome. The observed paused media index staying 7/15 after inline volume events is a concrete future proxy-parity case, not a behavior to hardcode for other devices.

## Reference backend eligibility decision — 7 November 2026

Retain **mountless evdev grab with uinput forwarding** as the reference candidate for later supervised passthrough validation. The measured USB HID collection is dedicated to buttons and separate from USB audio, phone controls and connection switches. Root-shell reads and the kernel’s evdev/uinput configuration support investigating this route. Active interception is ineligible until the real module service can open the required devices with SELinux enforcing and Android accepts the proxy with equivalent mappings, repeat behavior and allowed actions. The first physical worker also needs bounded pause and independent recovery. [Reference measurements](../discovery/reference-2026-11-04.md)

The measured profile candidate is the Xiaomi USB HID collection at interface `1.3`, with evdev bus/vendor/product/version `0003:0020:0b21:0201` and USB device revision `0100`. Its inspected capabilities include `EV_KEY` and `EV_MSC` with `MSC_SCAN`, with no axes or switches. The stable portion of USB/HID ancestry and the inspected capabilities matched across the controlled reconnect and later identity checks. Enrollment must combine measured attributes and reject ambiguous matches. Event numbers and allocation suffixes identify the current instance only. Private identity values stay in diagnostic evidence.

| Candidate group | Observed Linux key and scan | Physical Android mapping |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Center | `0x00a4` and `0x000c00cd` | `MEDIA_PLAY_PAUSE` |
| Volume Up | `0x0073` and `0x000c00e9` | `VOLUME_UP` |
| Volume Down | `0x0072` and `0x000c00ea` | `VOLUME_DOWN` |

These are the three observed controls. Other advertised keys need separate mapping evidence before entering a profile. Center remains a shared group. Disclose the reported call effects alongside the captured playback, assistant and call behavior. D11–D18 supply maintainer reports for specific call contexts. D09, D10 and D19 scope their respective discovery repetitions and baseline work. [Coverage decisions](../project-contract.md)

The scoped runtime choice is **inactive** while permissions and mapping remain unproven. Grab without forwarding would remove the full collection’s controls and cannot meet the selective passthrough requirement. A device-specific layout overlay would need independently proven layout scope, preservation and a mounting decision. A semantic adapter would introduce a separate framework compatibility and recovery gate. The existing evidence supports retaining the native candidate and the inactive outcome.

D20 waives the planned isolated incoming volume tap and connected-call microphone-route follow-up for Stage 1. Both remain Not run. The retained call snapshots show call mode and USB output patches with mix-port sources. The exact telephony microphone hardware path remains unresolved. The scoped Stage 1 exit permits Stage 2 host-side policy and identity work. The later service, proxy, passthrough and recovery checks belong to Stage 4. T08 and T11 still require physical evidence.
